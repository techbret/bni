# Product — BNI Torque Converter

## Business

**BNI Torque Converter** is an auto parts store that specializes in automotive rebuilders. They rebuild and supply **custom remanufactured torque converters** for automotive applications, with strong focus on:

- Classic vehicles
- Four-wheel-drive (4WD) applications
- Rebuilders and shops that need fitment-accurate converters and related parts information

The site is a **storefront + information catalog**: customers look up parts, learn about applications, and browse a heavy catalog of technical content—not a flashy consumer marketplace template.

## Primary customer jobs

1. **Find parts by vehicle** — search by make, model, and year (MMY)
2. **Browse by category** — navigate parts groups and automotive information from a side nav
3. **Inspect product detail** — SKU, specs, fitment, description, pricing
4. **Optional account** — log in later to view past orders and account details

## Auth and ordering

- Guests can browse the catalog and search without an account.
- Login and past orders are planned features; they are **not required to place an order** in the long-term product vision (guest checkout / inquiry flows may exist later).
- **Phase 1 does not require real auth, checkout, or payments.** Account and orders UIs may be stubbed with mock data for look-and-feel approval only.

## Tone and content

- Professional, technical, rebuild-oriented
- Clear fitment and specs over marketing fluff
- Dense catalog information should stay scannable (filters, sidebar, structured lists)

## Brand signals

- Name: **BNI Torque Converter** (hero-level brand presence on the storefront)
- Visual: orange and amber accents, light theme default with dark option — see [design-system.md](./design-system.md)
